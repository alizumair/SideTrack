Right Edge Slider: Smooth Volume Control

Slide your finger vertically along the rightmost 10% edge of your MacBook or Magic Trackpad. System volume adjusts with 64 ultra-smooth haptic micro-ticks. Double-tap the top-right corner to toggle Mute instantly.

💡 Tip: Hold ⇧⌥ (Shift+Option) while sliding for precision quarter-stepping.
